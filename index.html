Questions

Before you ask

Do you take cards?

No — the bakery is cash-only. Bring cash; several reviewers say the same, so come prepared.

When do the semitas come out?

Reviewers say fresh batches come out in the afternoon — after 2 PM, warm around 4:30 PM — and they sell out fast. Call ahead to check what is in the case.

Can I order a custom cake?

Yes — birthday cakes are a customer favorite. Order in advance by phone; one reviewer ordered two weeks ahead. Use the cake form on this site to build your request, then call to confirm.

Do you deliver?

No delivery — take-out and in-store pickup only.

Is parking easy?

Reviewers warn parking is packed, especially at peak hours between 5–7 PM — be patient, it is worth it.

What should I try first?

Reviewers point first-timers to the semitas, the picos, the pineapple honey cake and the strawberry sweet rolls.
